IMMEDIATE STEPS
───────────────
1. Open index.html in your browser → your site is live locally!

2. Add your profile photo:
   • Save your photo as: images/your-photo.jpg

3. Add project screenshots:
   • Save them as: images/project1.jpg, images/project2.jpg, etc.

4. Open index.html in a text editor and search for "EDIT:" to
   find every piece of content you should personalise.

5. To go live online → drag this whole folder onto netlify.com
   (free account, takes 30 seconds)

FULL GUIDE → See README.md for detailed instructions on:
   • Changing colours
   • Adding projects, experience, education
   • Making the contact form send real emails
   • Hosting on GitHub Pages, Netlify, Vercel, or cPanel

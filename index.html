ASOS Brand Stockout & Lost-Revenue Analysis

I analyzed an ASOS product dataset to identify which brands drive hidden revenue loss from frequent stockouts. I cleaned and prepared the data, extracted and standardized brand names from product descriptions, and filtered for high-signal brands with sufficient product volume. I calculated stockout metrics by parsing size-availability strings, then estimated “lost revenue” as stockout frequency × product price. I summarized results at the brand level (average price, average stockout rate, total lost revenue, product count) and created a scatterplot to highlight priority brands for restocking, liquidation, and inventory reallocation decisions.

Python Matplotlib Seaborn Pandas

SQL Sales Funnel Analysis

In this project I use SQL to move from raw event-level data to business insights. I used conditional aggregation to build funnel stages, calculated stage-to-stage conversion rates, compared acquisition channels, measured time between funnel events, and calculated revenue metrics such as average order value and revenue per buyer. The project helped me understand that SQL isn't just about retrieving data; it's about structuring data to answer specific business questions.
